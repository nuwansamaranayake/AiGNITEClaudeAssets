MODEL: seedance-2-0 (with lip-sync, single-pass audio, ~9 credits)
DURATION: 5 to 12 seconds typical
ASPECT_RATIO: 9:16 (vertical) or 1:1
DEFAULT_NEGATIVE: robotic delivery, off-sync lips, stiff posture, watermark

PROMPT:
A {{CHARACTER_DESCRIPTION}} says: "{{SPOKEN_LINE}}".
Setting: {{SETTING}}.
Mood: {{MOOD}}.
Camera: {{CAMERA_TYPE}}.
Wardrobe: {{WARDROBE}}.

NOTES:
- Quote the spoken line verbatim. Use natural punctuation.
- Keep the line under 12 seconds of spoken content for a 5-second clip.
- For multi-shot series with the same person, train a Soul Character first and use the character_id.
- Mood and camera at the end tune the delivery.

A thirty-five-year-old woman with warm features and shoulder-length dark hair says:

Setting: a sunlit home office with a brass elephant figurine on the desk.
Mood: genuine surprise tipping into delight.
Camera: handheld smartphone feel, three-quarter angle, eye-level.
Wardrobe: cream linen shirt, simple gold earrings.
